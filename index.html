About Angie

A life of faith expressed through music.

Angie Taylor is a Christian Music singer-songwriter from the Central FL area. From a young age she knew she wanted to write and sing music. Her sincere dedication and passionate heart for the Lord is what has drawn so many to her music.

“My purpose for singing and writing music is to point people to Christ. He alone deserves all the praise!”
